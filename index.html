Current work

Building spatial infrastructure for media

Asynthetic's work is published as it becomes real. The first is SIGMA, a desktop tool for working with large collections of visual media spatially rather than through folders and hierarchies.

Spatial media infrastructure

An infinite canvas for organizing, inspecting, and editing media

SIGMA is a local-first, Rust-backed spatial canvas where images and video are first-class objects you can arrange, crop, scrub, and loop across an effectively unbounded workspace, rather than a folder tree.

  • Infinite spatial canvas
  • Media as first-class spatial objects
  • Frame-accurate video interaction
  • Crop, resize, A/B loops
  • Saved spatial workflows
  • Local-first Rust media pipeline
  • Lightweight Tauri desktop shell
  • ComfyUI integration (in progress)

Approach

What connects the work

Four principles guide how we build.

01

Human control

Interfaces should make human control clear as systems become more autonomous, not bury it behind automation.

02

Spatial systems

Information and machine state can often be understood more effectively spatially than through traditional linear interfaces.

03

Local capability

Where practical, computation and data remain local, inspectable, and under the operator's control.

04

Verification

Autonomy should be paired with deterministic interfaces, observability, testing, and human review.

About

An independent engineering studio

Asynthetic builds interfaces and infrastructure for the boundary between people and increasingly autonomous machines. As software becomes easier for machines to produce, the harder problem becomes keeping the resulting systems coherent, inspectable, and under human control.
